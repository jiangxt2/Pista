# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Pista contributors should make technical collaboration safe, respectful, and useful. Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people. Welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes, give actionable feedback, and respect differences in experience, identity, language, and perspective.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community events, and other spaces where a participant represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers will consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected people. They will avoid conflicts of interest and limit disclosure to what is necessary for a fair response.

## Good-faith technical disagreement

Firm disagreement about architecture, correctness, compatibility, or project scope is allowed. Keep it evidence-based, stay on topic, and follow a maintainer decision once the decision is recorded.
